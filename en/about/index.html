How I work

The plan comes first. The paperwork comes after.

  • Clarity

    Plain explanations, no legalese. Official terms always translated into language people actually speak.

  • Strategy

    A plan built for your profile: what kind of role to target, which permit to apply for, and how long until Stamp 4.

  • Security

    Decisions grounded in the law and in how the Irish market actually works, not in what gets passed around online.

Before we talk

This path takes preparation, and it isn't for everyone

I work with professionals who

  • Have between 3 and 10 years of professional experience
  • Work in fields that are eligible in the Irish market
  • See immigration as a career investment
  • Want to build a real life here, not a temporary plan

What I don't do

  • Illegal shortcuts or promises of bending the rules
  • Tourism and recreational exchange programmes
  • Off-the-cuff guidance, with no plan and no preparation

Irish authorities talk to each other, and the wrong decision carries a high price. My commitment is to protect your career, not to feed illusions.
